                                                   1.1-Intro

This FAQ is a list of treasure dropped by the enemies in Dragon Warrior 3. The
list of treasure dropped is in alphabetical order with the enemies listed that
drop the treasure. I started the idea for a FAQ, but xiro was the one who gave
me all the info since he has gotten almost all the treasure and I would have
taken a bit of time compiling the list of treasures I've gotten. All but the
Man-Eater chest and the bosses have treasure, so they aren't listed. I myself
have gotten a lot of treasure, but not as much as xiro has. This is a complete
list of treasure that is dropped. It is possible the Man-Eater Chest drops
treasure, but the answer to that remains unknown.

                                1.5-The enemies that drop nothing section

These enemies have not been confirmed to drop anything. They are as follows:

Man-Eater Chest
Kandar Henchman
Kandar (second battle)
Baramos
King Hydra
Baramos Bomus
Baramos Gonus
Zoma

Shadow (These guys mimic an enemy. They mimic attack,defense,HP,spells,moves,
treasure dropped, all. They will drop treasure. One time I was fighting a
shadow that mimicked a marine slime. It said "Marine Slime dropped a treasure
chest" and it was a wing of wyvern. So they only drop the treasure of the enemy
it mimics)
